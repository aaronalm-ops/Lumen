Personal media player for Android

Lumen

Lumen is a personal, ad-free media player for long-form audio such as sermons, and for music and video. It never loses your place: every file remembers exactly where you stopped, even after the phone restarts.

What it does

  • Plays audio and video stored on your phone.
  • Streams files from a Google Drive folder that you pick yourself, and can keep them for offline listening.
  • Equaliser presets, speed control, sleep timer, bookmarks, labels and playlists.

Who it is for

Lumen is a private project built by Aaron Almeida for his own devices and a small number of family devices. It is not distributed through Google Play.
