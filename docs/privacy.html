Lumen Privacy Policy

Lumen for Android (com.aaronalmeida.lumen). Effective 23 October 2026, version 0.3.3.

Lumen is a personal media player for long spoken-word recordings, such as sermons, and the music beside them. It is made by Aaron Almeida for private and family use. This page explains what Lumen reads, what it keeps and where anything goes.

The short version

  • Lumen has no account system, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporter.
  • Everything Lumen keeps (your library, playback positions, labels, bookmarks and settings) stays on your phone.
  • Lumen never sells, shares or transfers your data to anyone.
  • With Google Drive connected, Lumen only reads your Drive to list and play the audio and video in the folder you choose. It never modifies or deletes anything there.

Files on your phone

When you tap "Scan whole phone", Lumen asks Android for permission to read audio and video files and reads the index Android already keeps (titles, durations, sizes, folders and, for music, the artist and embedded artwork). When you add a folder, Lumen reads only that folder. This information is stored in Lumen's own database on your phone so it can show your library and remember your place in every file.

Google Drive

Connecting Google Drive is optional. When you choose a Drive folder in Settings, Lumen asks Google for two permissions:

  • Read-only access to your Drive (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.readonly). Lumen uses it to show your folders so you can choose one, and to list and play the audio and video in the folder you choose and its subfolders. Lumen never modifies, moves or deletes anything in your Drive.
  • Access to files Lumen creates (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file). Lumen uses it only for its own backup files: it creates a folder called "Lumen" in your Drive and saves its backup files there. It cannot change any other file with this permission.

From Drive, Lumen reads folder names, file names, file types, sizes and dates, the media content of the files you play or keep offline, and your Google account's email address, which is shown in Settings so you can see which account is connected. File details are stored in Lumen's database on your phone. Files you play are cached on your phone (up to 200 MB) and files you choose to keep offline are saved on your phone until you remove them or disconnect Drive.

Lumen talks to Google directly from your phone. There is no Lumen server, and nothing from your Drive passes through anyone else.

To stop Lumen's access, tap Disconnect in Settings, Google Drive (this removes the Drive entries and offline copies from your phone), and remove Lumen at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Lumen's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

In particular, Lumen uses Google user data only to provide the features described above, does not transfer it to anyone, does not use it for advertising, and does not allow any person to read it. Nobody other than you sees your Drive data through Lumen.

Backups

"Back up" writes your positions, labels, bookmarks and settings to a file in a place you choose. When Drive is connected, the same file is also saved in the "Lumen" folder in your Drive, and a fresh copy is saved there about once a week. "Restore" reads a backup file you choose and merges it in; it never deletes anything.

Diagnostics

"Share diagnostics" in Settings builds a file with Lumen's recent log, the state of its database and device details (such as the Android version and storage permissions), and opens Android's share sheet. Nothing is sent unless you choose where to send it.

Update check

Once a day Lumen asks GitHub (api.github.com) whether a newer release exists. The request carries no personal data beyond what any web request does, such as your IP address, which GitHub handles under its own privacy statement.

Children

Lumen has a lock for children's playback but is not directed at children and collects nothing from them.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling Lumen deletes everything it stored on your phone. Backup files you saved, and the "Lumen" folder in your Drive, stay where you put them until you delete them.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date. Questions: open an issue at github.com/aaronalm-ops/lumen.

Lumen is a private app for personal use and has not been verified by Google.
