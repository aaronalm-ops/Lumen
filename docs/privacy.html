Lumen · Privacy policy · Last updated 22 October 2026

Privacy policy

Lumen is a personal media player. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no servers of its own. Everything it knows stays on your phone unless you choose to back it up.

Files on your phone

With your permission, Lumen reads the audio and video files on your device to list and play them. File names, durations and artwork are stored in a database on your phone only.

Google Drive

If you connect Google Drive, Lumen asks for the drive.file permission. This lets it see only the folders and files you pick in Google's own picker, and files Lumen itself creates (your backups). It cannot see the rest of your Drive. Lumen uses this access only to list, stream and download the files you chose, and to save backups you asked for. Drive data is not shared with anyone and is not used for any other purpose. Lumen's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What is stored, and where

  • Playback positions, bookmarks, labels and settings: on your phone.
  • Backups: only where you choose to save them, and in a "Lumen" folder in your Drive if Drive is connected.
  • Diagnostics: a log file created on your phone only when you tap Share diagnostics, sent only where you choose to send it.

Network use

Besides Google Drive, Lumen contacts GitHub once a day to check whether a newer version of the app exists. No personal information is sent.

Removing your data

Uninstalling Lumen deletes everything it stored on the phone. You can disconnect Drive at any time in Lumen's settings or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
